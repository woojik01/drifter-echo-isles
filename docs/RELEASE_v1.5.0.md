# DRIFTER · Echo Isles v1.5.0

Based on the uploaded v1.4.0 build. The original build was published first.

- Gathering completes its lesson only after a resource is granted to the bag, in solo and co-op. Magnet movement now covers the full pickup radius and shows a collection receipt on desktop and touch devices.
- An optional first camp project costs 4 wood and 2 stone. The personal lantern persists in the current journey and unlocks three deterministic trail recipes. Its prompt can be dismissed; no island route is forced.
- Trail recipes guarantee Ore Echo, Mistwalker or Wayfinder. Empty slots equip a crafted item; full equipment opens comparison in the bag. Shared party progression and personal camp decoration remain separate.
- Basic and advanced recipes remain available in an expandable section. Costs display current/required resources and sources of missing materials.
- Ground equipment previews changes before equipping, with signed units, slot comparisons and explicit gained/lost effects.
- Island plants grow through sprout, growing and bloom stages over 90 seconds of active play. Existing planted gardens migrate as mature blooms.
- Surviving at least 60 seconds of a night and reaching dawn leaves a persistent cosmetic firefly at camp. Dawn receipts cannot repeat after reload. Day/night lengths and campfire defense are preserved.
- Korean and English copy and small landscape layouts were updated. All changes preserve the existing pixel art style and local asset bundle.

Validation: syntax checks; existing gameplay, input, save/import and SDK regression suites; nine new camp/collection/gear/growth cases; authoritative co-op grant replay and shared/personal progress checks; actual Canvas renderer at 800x450 and 1280x720; Worker artifact and route tests.

The managed browser preview supervisor was unavailable in this environment. Browser layout and a live two-player network session were not verified in this run.
